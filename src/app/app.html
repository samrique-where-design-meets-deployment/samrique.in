Zipi Bike Taxi is an on-demand mobility platform engineered in Flutter to make urban transit fast, affordable, and reliable. It encompasses a dual-role architectural model: commuters enjoy seamless pickup requests, real-time captain tracking, and ride invoices, while captain partners benefit from order radar, route navigation, and daily earnings analytics.

The solution integrates complete captain document verification (KYC), vehicle selection, and multi-language localization to serve riders and drivers across metropolitan transit zones.
